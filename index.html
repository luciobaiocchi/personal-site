About me

Hi! I'm a Computer Science student at Politecnico di Torino. I like building things from scratch, especially when the work crosses a boundary between software and the real world. Lately that has meant anomaly detection for a bakery, order tooling for a food distributor, and open-source security software for coding agents.

I'm increasingly fascinated by distributed systems: how separate pieces coordinate, fail, and recover. In my free time I'm building a simulated environment for testing distributed frameworks under more realistic conditions. When I'm not at a keyboard, you'll usually find me running or lifting at the gym. 🤙
